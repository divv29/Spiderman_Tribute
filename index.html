About Peter Parker

Peter Parker, portrayed by Tom Holland in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, is a high school student who gains spider-like abilities after being bitten by a radioactive spider.

As Spider-Man, Peter struggles to balance his life as a superhero with his responsibilities as a teenager, all while learning what it truly means to be a hero under the mentorship of Tony Stark.

Throughout his journey in the MCU, Peter evolves from an eager young hero to someone who understands that "with great power comes great responsibility."

Key Moments in the MCU

2016 - Civil War

Peter is recruited by Tony Stark to help in the conflict between the Avengers. He makes his first appearance as Spider-Man, impressing everyone with his abilities.

2017 - Homecoming

Peter tries to prove himself as more than just a "friendly neighborhood Spider-Man" while dealing with the Vulture and high school life.

2018 - Infinity War

Peter joins the fight against Thanos on Titan. He is one of the victims of the Snap, disappearing in Tony Stark's arms.

2019 - Endgame

Peter returns after the Blip and participates in the final battle against Thanos. He shares an emotional reunion with Tony Stark.

2019 - Far From Home

Peter deals with the loss of Tony Stark while on a school trip to Europe, where he faces Mysterio and is framed for his murder.

2021 - No Way Home

After his identity is revealed to the world, Peter seeks Doctor Strange's help, leading to a multiversal conflict with past Spider-Man villains.
